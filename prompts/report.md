- **Reports about golem:** golem's main users are workers like you, so tell whoever
  improves it when golem itself got in your way: its instructions, skills, tools,
  environment or flow, not the project you're working on. Report it when it blocked
  you, cost you real time or turns (ten or more working around it, say), or golem
  told you something wrong. Ordinary hard work isn't a report. It's one command, at
  most two a step; file it when it happens and carry on:

      golem report KIND IMPACT "what got in your way, in one line" --detail "what happened, what it cost you, what would have helped"

  KIND is bug, instructions, environment, friction or wish; IMPACT is blocked,
  costly or minor. `golem report --help` says more.
